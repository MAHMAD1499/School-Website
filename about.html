Our Story

Kindergarten Saadia's Montessori School was founded in 2009 by a group of passionate educators and parents who believed deeply in the transformative power of the Montessori method. What began as a small classroom of eight children in Haripur has grown into a thriving learning community serving over 150 students across Early Toddler, Primary/Kindergarten, and Junior Level programs.

Our school was built on the belief, echoed in the words of Dr. Maria Montessori herself: "Free the child's potential and you will transform him into the world." We bring this philosophy to life through beautifully prepared environments, trained AMI guides, and a deep respect for each child's unique developmental journey.

Our Core Values

Every decision we make is guided by four foundational values that define who we are.

Safety & Trust

We create warm, structured spaces where children feel secure enough to take risks, ask questions, and express themselves freely.

Curiosity & Discovery

We ignite and protect children's natural curiosity, providing materials and experiences that deepen wonder rather than replace it.

Community & Respect

Multi-age classrooms and collaborative projects teach children mutual respect, leadership, and empathy for one another.

Excellence & Growth

We hold high standards for every student, guide, and parent interaction — continuously learning and improving together.

Our History

From humble beginnings to a flourishing school community, our journey reflects our commitment to growth.

2009

School Founded

KSM opens its doors to its first 8 students in a single-room setting in Haripur.

2012

Primary Program Launched

Growing demand leads to the launch of our Primary/Kindergarten program for ages 3–6.

2015

New Campus Opening

We move to our current, purpose-built campus designed specifically for Montessori environments.

2018

AMI Certification Achieved

All core teaching staff complete AMI (Association Montessori Internationale) training.

2022

Junior Level Program Added

We extend our offering to include the Junior Level program for ages 6–9, completing the full cycle.

2026

150+ Students Strong

Today, KSM Haripur proudly serves over 150 students with 24 certified educators.
