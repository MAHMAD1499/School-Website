Kindergarten Saadia's Montessori School

Nurturing independent, confident, and lifelong learners using the authentic Montessori method since 2010. We believe every child deserves a joyful, purposeful, and beautiful learning environment.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide an authentic Montessori education that fosters the natural development of each child's intellectual, social, emotional, and physical potential, empowering them to become self-directed, life-long learners.

🔭

Our Vision

To be the leading Montessori institution in the region, renowned for excellence in early childhood education, nurturing a generation of compassionate, creative, and capable global citizens.

📖 Our Story

Founded in 2010 by Ms. Saadia Khan, a certified AMI Montessori educator, Kindergarten Saadia's Montessori School began as a small classroom of just 15 students. With a deep passion for early childhood education and the authentic Montessori philosophy, Ms. Saadia transformed the school into a thriving learning community.

Over the years, the school has grown to serve over 200 students across three distinct programs — Early Childhood (ages 2.5–6), Kindergarten (ages 5–7), and Junior Level (ages 6–9). Each program is carefully designed to respect the child's natural developmental stages.

Today, KSM is proud to hold renewed AMI certification, representing our unwavering commitment to the highest standards of Montessori education.
